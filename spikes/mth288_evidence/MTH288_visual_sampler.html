DM_Counting · Generator 1 · dm_count_product_rule

Question: A student has \(4\) shirts, \(3\) pairs of pants, and \(4\) pairs of shoes. How many outfits choose one of each?

Answer: \(48\)

Worked steps
  1. Apply the product rule because one independent choice is made from each category.
  2. \(4\cdot3\cdot4=48\).
Curated mistake choices
  • \(11\) — The sum rule is not used when all three choices are made together.
  • \(16\) — All independent stages must be multiplied.
